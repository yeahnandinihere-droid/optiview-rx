**Research Purpose and Future Vision**

OptiView-Rx is an experimental research project investigating whether personalised computational optical compensation can enable people with refractive errors to read on conventional smartphones without wearing spectacles.

The long-term objective is to develop a software-based solution that, if successfully developed and scientifically validated, could potentially reduce the need for spectacles specifically during everyday smartphone reading.

This technology is currently under research and development. Its ability to provide effective spectacle-free reading has not yet been established.
